Join Legacies

Legacies Global partners with artisan designers and cultural innovators to produce curated editions and sustainable collaborations. If you are looking to apply to join a fashion brand through a thoughtful creative partnership, this is where it starts.

Who we're looking for

We welcome designers, workshops and creative studios with a clear point of view and a considered approach to making. An artisan designer application is a way for us to learn about your work and what you want to explore next. We are interested in practice, not polish for its own sake: how you work, what informs your choices and what kind of partnership would feel useful to you.

  • Designers and ateliers with a distinct cultural or artisanal point of view
  • Makers committed to quality and sustainable practice
  • Studios ready to collaborate on curated editions
  • Creators who want their story told with care

Legacies Global works across borders and welcomes applications from partners worldwide. We are particularly interested in conversations with African artisan designers and other independent makers whose work carries a perspective that deserves a wider audience. We do not expect every applicant to be an established label; a thoughtful portfolio and a clear sense of purpose are more useful than an impressive claim.

What to prepare

Before you begin, gather a link to your portfolio, website or Instagram; a short bio about you and your craft; a few images or samples of recent work; and a sentence or two about why you would like to work with Legacies. Your sample link is optional in the form, but a portfolio link helps us understand your practice. Please share only material you have permission to show.

The form is an invitation to start a conversation, not a promise of selection or commercial work. There is no need to send original objects or confidential files at this stage. If you have accessibility needs or prefer another way to share information, write to legacies@legacies.global.

What happens next

You submit

Share your details and a concise introduction to your work through the secure form below.

We review

Our team reviews every application personally, considering the practice and the information you provide.

We connect

If there may be a fit, we reach out by email to learn more and talk through your goals.

We shape it together

If we move forward, we agree the scope, credit, timing and working terms with you.

Review timing can vary with the volume and nature of applications. We do not promise a response date or a place in a collection. If you have not heard back and would like an update, contact us by email.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be an established brand?

No. Emerging designers are welcome. We care about craft and point of view.

Where are you based?

Legacies Global works with partners worldwide.

What if I don't hear back?

Email legacies@legacies.global and we'll check on it.
