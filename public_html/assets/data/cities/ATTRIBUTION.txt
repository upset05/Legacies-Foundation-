City and administrative-place names are derived from the GeoNames Gazetteer
through the cities.json dataset (https://github.com/lutangar/cities.json).
The dataset includes populated places with a population over 1,000 and
administrative seats down to PPLA3; it is licensed under CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

The country names and ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes are generated with country-list
(https://github.com/fannarsh/country-list), licensed under the MIT License.
The source datasets are bundled by country in this directory at build time.
